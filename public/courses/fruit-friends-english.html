      For teachers and parents
      • Every unit has the same six steps: Story, Words, Match, Build, Quiz and Create. Each unit takes about 30 minutes and splits easily across two lessons.
      • Unit 1 focuses on adjectives for taste and touch. Unit 2 focuses on food words and “like / don’t like” sentences and questions. Unit 3 focuses on words for where things are (in, on, under…) and “Where is…?” questions. Unit 4 focuses on action verbs with “can / can’t” and “Can you…?” questions. Unit 5 focuses on number words from 12 to 100 and “How many…?”. It assumes the children already know 1 to 10, and it gives extra practice with thirteen / thirty and fifteen / fifty. Unit 6 focuses on daily routines, telling the time (o’clock and half past) and the -s / -es ending for he / she. Unit 7 focuses on the past simple: regular -ed verbs, common irregular verbs, “didn’t + base verb” and “Did you…?” questions. Unit 8 focuses on weather words, “What’s the weather like?” and what to wear in each kind of weather. Unit 9 is a review party. It uses one word from each earlier unit, shows every key sentence pattern again, and ends with a graduation card.
      • Pair idea: read the story aloud, one line each. After the last step, partners read their fruit friend cards to each other and guess the picture.
      • Extension: ask each child to bring one real fruit (Unit 1) or draw their own picnic basket (Unit 2) and describe it in English.
